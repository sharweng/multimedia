Rotoscoping Progress

Follow my comprehensive journey through the art of rotoscoping from week 3 to week 13. Each video showcases the progression of skills and techniques learned throughout this creative process.

The Rotoscoping Process

1

Video Reference Preparation

The first step involves selecting and preparing the video reference. This includes choosing the right footage and adjusting frame rates for optimal rotoscoping.

2

Rotoscoping Each Frame

Individual frames are carefully rotoscoped from the video. The background is replaced with solid green color for easier background replacement later.

3

Chroma Key

The green background is removed using chroma key techniques, isolating the subject for further processing.

4

Tricolor Application

The rotoscoped subject is converted to tricolor format with light, medium, and dark shades to easily apply pencil effects later.

5

Pencil Effect

Multiple effects are applied in After Effects to transform the tricolored frames from solid colors to realistic pencil textures.

6

Final Edits

The final step involves adding the original video for reference and including personal branding elements.
